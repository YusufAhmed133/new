## 1. Holistic mark first: senior contracts academic, rubric set aside

**Mark: 85/100. A high Distinction, not in the 90s.**

This is a serious, legally literate advice. It reads the facts closely and is well organised by client claim. The doctrinal choices are almost all right:
- Gibson is distinguished on its words.
- Masters is used through Jessel MR's Winn v Bull passage for a qualifier that mentions no formal contract, and the analogy is disclosed.
- Meehan is distinguished on Gibbs CJ's own limit, not on its result, and the honesty fetter is put and met.

Few undergraduate answers reach this level of authority control.

**Why it is not 90 or above on a hard reading:**
1. **Density over explanation.** Several steps are asserted in compressed or elliptical prose, and a reader has to reconstruct the logic:
   - "though no formal document was contemplated, so did the email"
   - "Grace's promises were made to AMPL, not Rocky, even as joint promisee"
   - "taking the $10,000 promise knowing its terms"

   The many two-to-four-word quotations (244 quoted words in 69 sentences) sometimes read as quote-stitching rather than analysis.
2. **One internal contradiction in the consideration paragraph** (section 3, defect C1). The clause "bound no one before signing", yet the verdict turns on whether "the clause preceded the bargain".
3. **Some leans are reasoned only negatively.** The 12 months is the main example: AMPL's reasons are neutralised, but no positive reason is given for 8 April.
4. **Some authorities are stretched slightly:**
   - Gibbs J's "Perhaps" is said to have "likewise assumed" the reasonableness standard.
   - Mason J's "at least when" is turned into a limit on the rule.
   - Deane J's bare agency sentence is cited for a two-element test.
5. **Missed or thin points a contracts academic expects:**
   - the Banque Brussels timing difference (the facility there was conditioned on the letter; here AMPL had already advanced);
   - the price of Grace's promises in the Loan Agreement;
   - a reason for reading "the clause" as the guarantee clause;
   - Lord Greene MR's "when and how they contemplate becoming bound" for AMPL's "nothing until signing" point.
6. **Length.** Against the Instructions it is 99 words over. A UNSW marker applying the Instructions would penalise that whatever the quality (section 5).
