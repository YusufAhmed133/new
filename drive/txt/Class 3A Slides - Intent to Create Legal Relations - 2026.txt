• Category 1 – parties have reached finality in all parts of their
   bargain and intend to be bound with no changes
   – Immediately binding
• Category 2 – parties have agreed all terms but made one or
  more terms conditional on execution of contract
   – Import a binding obligation to have a binding contract
   – Once the formal contract exists they will be bound
• Category 3 – no bargain intended until a formal contract is
  signed, satisfactory to my solicitors “subject to contract” (M v
  C)
   – Not binding at all

                                   Delaney v Delaney (2022) VSCA 48
                                                                        Intention to Create Legal Relations

•   Sean and Patrick, owned a group of companies and trusts
•   Their relationship deteriorated
•   Sean fired Patrick
•   Sean and Patrick executed a heads of agreement. In broad terms, the agreement
    provided for Sean to buy Patrick out of the group for $10 million. It also provided
    for further negotiations (including as to tax issues) and allowed a short period of
    time in which a more formal agreement might be concluded.
•   No further agreement was concluded. Within weeks, Patrick purported to
    terminate the agreement.
•   Incomplete Heads of Agreement
•   Tax details of deal not resolved
• [60] The first inquiry is whether agreement has been reached on the essential terms
    constitutive of a contract; the second inquiry is whether the parties have (by agreeing on
    enough other important terms) expressed an intention to be bound by those terms.. These
    are distinct inquiries and may yield different answers. The parties may intend to make a
    contract but fail to do so by not agreeing on the essential terms. Conversely, the parties may
    have reached agreement on the essential terms but not yet intend to be bound.
•   [78] In all the circumstances, the trial judge was correct in our opinion to find that it was
    established that the parties to the heads of agreement intended to enter into a binding
    agreement to complete the sale and purchase transaction.
